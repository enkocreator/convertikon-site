Installing on a Mac

  1. Open the downloaded convertikon-2.2.0-mac.dmg. A window opens with an arrow.
  2. Drag CONVERTIKON onto the Applications folder.
  3. Eject the disk (the ⏏ next to CONVERTIKON in the Finder sidebar).
  4. Open CONVERTIKON from Applications. The first time, macOS may say the app was downloaded from the internet. Click Open. The app is signed and notarised by Apple, so there's no other warning.

If you open the app straight from the disk window instead, it offers to move itself to Applications. Click Move to Applications Folder. Running it from the disk is what stops the disk from ejecting.

CONVERTIKON runs on Macs with Apple Silicon and Intel, macOS 10.15 Catalina or later.

Installing on Windows

There are two files. Either works; you don't need both.

  • convertikon-2.2.0-windows-setup.exe installs the app, with shortcuts on the desktop and in the Start menu. Run it and follow the steps.
  • convertikon-2.2.0-windows-portable.exe needs no installation. Put it anywhere and double-click it.

"Windows protected your PC"

On first run, Windows may show a blue box saying Windows protected your PC, from a feature called SmartScreen. It appears for any new app that hasn't yet built up a download history with Microsoft, and it is not saying the file is harmful. To continue: click More info, then Run anyway. You only have to do this once.

Two ways to be sure you have the real file: download it only from convertikon.app or the link in your receipt, and check that Elizabeth Enko is listed as the publisher in the file's Properties → Details.

CONVERTIKON runs on Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit).

Typing feet and inches

Type into any field and the other four follow. In the FT field you can write feet and inches almost any way you'd say them:

You typeIt means
66 feet
6-36 feet 3 inches
6'36 feet 3 inches
6 36 feet 3 inches (a space works when whole inches follow)
6'-3 1/2"6 feet 3½ inches, the way it's written on drawings
6'3.56 feet 3½ inches
164 0 1/2164 feet ½ inch

In the IN field, type inches with a fraction (75 1/2) or a decimal (75.5). Fractions are shown to the nearest 1/16".

FRACTIONAL shows feet-and-inches with fractions (6'-3 1/2"); DECIMAL keeps feet-and-inches but writes the inches as a decimal (6'-3.5", 75.5), and there you can also type decimal feet such as 6.25. Click the grey word at the bottom right to switch; the app remembers your choice.

A comma works as a decimal point (1,5), for keyboards whose number pad types one.

Handy keys

  • Click a field: its value is selected, so you can type straight over it. Click again to place the cursor.
  • Double-click a value to copy it. COPIED appears for a moment.
  • Enter finishes the field. Esc clears all five.
  • The pin (bottom left) keeps the window on top of your other apps.
  • The icon in the menu bar (Mac) or tray (Windows) shows and hides the window. Right-click it to take the app out of the Dock or taskbar, or to quit.
